How we estimate this

We add up the new spend on each bill you pay here since your card-year began (March), minus balances carried over and anything you mark as not counting.

Refunds, EMIs and excluded categories inside a bill aren’t visible to us, so we call it an estimate. Paytm co-brand cards show exact figures from the issuer.
